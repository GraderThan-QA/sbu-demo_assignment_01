The main thing I learned is the idea of a module versus an interface: bill.py holds
the four functions (tip_amount, grand_total, split_evenly, is_generous) and the
console, notebook, and Streamlit files just import them. Because the unit tests
call the functions directly, test_split_evenly passing told me the math was right
before I touched any interface — that is the point of separating the two.

My first submission failed test_streamlit_app with a KeyError even though the app
looked correct in the browser. Reading the test showed me why: AppTest looks up
widgets by their key= argument (key="subtotal", key="tip", key="people") and reads
results from st.metric tiles, and I had used st.write with no keys. So the
integration test was checking the *structure* of my interface, not just the numbers.
Adding the keys and switching to st.columns + st.metric fixed it.
